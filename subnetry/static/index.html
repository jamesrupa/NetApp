    Port Scanner (Nmap)

    Find open ports and the software behind them using Nmap: on your LAN or on public addresses you own. Only scan systems you own or are allowed to test.

    Public target: scanning from inside your own network can show more than the internet sees, because some routers loop the request back internally. To see exactly what's exposed, scan from another connection (e.g. a phone hotspot).

    Choose a target and scan type, then press Run Nmap.

    Traffic Analyzer

    Capture packets with Wireshark's engine (tshark) and see what's happening in plain English.

    Pick the interface you use to reach the internet (usually Wi-Fi or Ethernet) and start a capture.

    What can I see?

    On Wi-Fi and modern switched networks, a computer only sees its own traffic plus broadcast/multicast chatter from other devices (device announcements, DHCP, ARP). To watch every device you need a switch with a mirror/SPAN port, a Wi-Fi adapter in monitor mode, or to capture on the router itself. Encrypted traffic (HTTPS, QUIC) only reveals which site was contacted, not what was sent.
